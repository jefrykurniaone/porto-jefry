# Porto-Jefry

A bilingual (EN/ID) personal portfolio that presents one developer's experience, projects, skills and education to recruiters and clients, on the web and as a generated PDF CV.

## Language

### Page composition

**Hero stage**:
The opening screen, held in place while the visitor scrolls through its sequence and then released into the rest of the page.
_Avoid_: splash, intro, landing

**Role ticker**:
The line in the hero stage that types out one role at a time, erases it, and moves to the next.
_Avoid_: running text, typed roles, tagline

**Manifesto**:
The About section presented as one oversized statement over the dot field, followed by the About prose and the profile photo.
_Avoid_: statement section, intro text

**Marquee**:
A continuously scrolling horizontal strip of tech-stack and company names, set as text rather than logos.
_Avoid_: running text, ticker, logo wall, client strip

**Color block**:
A full-bleed section whose background departs from the page's theme background.
_Avoid_: band, panel

### Text

**Reading text**:
Prose a visitor reads in full: the About paragraphs, experience bullets and project descriptions. Held to a higher contrast than any other text, with nothing decorative moving behind it.
_Avoid_: body copy, paragraph text

**Label**:
Short supporting text read at a glance, such as a period, a company name, a kicker or a tag.
_Avoid_: caption, meta, muted text

### Motion

**Dot field**:
The animated particle tunnel behind the manifesto, drifting toward the viewer and leaning toward the cursor.
_Avoid_: particle canvas, starfield, background animation

**Ink flip**:
The header's text colour switching so it stays legible over whichever color block is beneath it.
_Avoid_: header theme, nav colour change

**Reveal**:
Content entering the first time it scrolls into view.
_Avoid_: fade-in, on-scroll animation

**Reduced-motion fallback**:
The still rendering that every motion effect has when the visitor has asked their system for reduced motion.
_Avoid_: static mode, no-animation mode
