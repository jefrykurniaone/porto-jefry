# Porto-Jefry

A bilingual (EN/ID) personal portfolio that presents one developer's experience, projects, skills and education to recruiters and clients, on the web and as a generated PDF CV.

## Language

### Page composition

**Role ticker**:
The line in the hero that types out one role at a time, erases it, and moves to the next.
_Avoid_: running text, typed roles, tagline

### Text

**Reading text**:
Prose a visitor reads in full: the About paragraphs, experience bullets and project descriptions. Held to a higher contrast than any other text, with nothing decorative moving behind it.
_Avoid_: body copy, paragraph text

**Label**:
Short supporting text read at a glance, such as a period, a company name, a kicker or a tag.
_Avoid_: caption, meta, muted text

### Motion

**Reduced-motion fallback**:
The still rendering that every motion effect has when the visitor has asked their system for reduced motion.
_Avoid_: static mode, no-animation mode
